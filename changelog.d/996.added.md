The crewboard TUI has a read-only front desk view (`m` or `--desk`) showing the iMessage bridge state, pending, sent and failed bubbles, retries, oldest pending age and desk reply latency, plus the recent turns: each inbound text, its route and the desk reply. `launch` and `crewship update` open a `frontdesk` herdr tab for it, under the `crewboard_tab` switch.
