The `instagram-dm` skill now has a reel curator, `reel_curator.py`, and a user timer `instagram-reel-curator.timer` that runs it three times a day: it pulls new reels from the accounts and hashtags in a host config file, skips any reel already logged or in the operator's thread, transcribes them with the `watch` skill, scores them with one `omp -p` call, rejects engagement bait, and shares at most three a day with one line (what, repo link, why), logging every pick and rejection in `reels.jsonl` (#403).
