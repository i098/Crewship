The worker image drops the package-manager caches (npm, uv, bun, pip, cargo), the rustup docs, the onnxruntime-node binaries for other operating systems and CPU architectures, and the onnxruntime CUDA and TensorRT providers, which makes it smaller with the same tools; `.dockerignore` also keeps `docs/`, `changelog.d/` and all tests except `tests/container-smoke.sh` out of the build context.
