At CRIT the storage guard also removes gitignored build output (`node_modules`, `.venv`, `target`, `dist`, `.next`, ...) that nothing changed for 7 days in Treehouse pool worktrees, with `fdu --ignored only` when the opt-in `fdu` profile installed it and with `find` otherwise. It never touches tracked files, uncommitted or unpushed work, or paths outside the pools, and `worktree-prune.sh --dry-run` lists what it would remove.
