The iMessage bridge now sends one request-specific acknowledgement when a delayed handoff has no answer, clears expired typing, and records transport acceptance separately from queued responses.
Targeted replies preserve older unanswered requests, chat deadlines do not interrupt another chat's draft, and restart recovery has no age cutoff.
Synchronous inbox commands remain limited to five seconds each and can delay acknowledgement and typing timers.
Cancelled, unsent acknowledgements remain recoverable after restart, and initial card replay confirms acceptance without treating later edits as new answers.
Uncertain acknowledgements check delivery history before cancellation, and cross-chat desk answers preserve other chats' unanswered requests.
Explicit replies include current edits, and acknowledgements clear typing without waiting for active mate delivery.
Known Photon edits retain the original chat reference when raw events use a different chat identifier.
See [the routing and typing contract](docs/imessage.md#what-it-does) for timing, trace fields, and recovery limits.
