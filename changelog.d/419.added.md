`fm-imessage --card` sends an iMessage app card: a `choice` card whose tap files `[iMessage card pick] <key>: <choice>` with the Firstmate inbox and edits the bubble in place, or a `bars`, `timeline` or `table` card drawn on its page. A card-only listener serves the pages by single-use, expiring tokens behind a named Cloudflare tunnel (`crewship.imessage.cards`).
