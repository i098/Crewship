`crewship.ci_pool.nice` lowers the CPU priority of CI: the slots get that `Nice=`, and job containers start in `ci.slice` with a lower `CPUWeight` and no CPU cap, so CI yields the CPU to agents and interactive work and still uses idle CPU. A slot that apply cannot show idle keeps its job; a later apply or `ci-pool.py watch` restarts it into the new `pool.json` when a fresh read shows it idle.
