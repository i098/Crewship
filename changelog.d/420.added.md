The opt-in `forgejo` profile (`crewship.profiles.forgejo`) runs a self-hosted Forgejo; [Self-hosted Forgejo](docs/forgejo.md) describes it. The recipe (`blueprint/ansible/tasks/forgejo.yml`) runs the latest Forgejo release on `127.0.0.1:20250` with SQLite after its checksum and its release-key signature match. It publishes Forgejo to the tailnet over HTTPS when Tailscale runs with HTTPS certificates enabled, and otherwise keeps the loopback URL. It creates an admin account with a host-local password, and dumps Forgejo every night with a copy of its secret files, which a restore needs.
