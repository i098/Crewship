A Forgejo recipe (`blueprint/ansible/tasks/forgejo.yml`) runs the latest Forgejo release on `127.0.0.1:20250` with SQLite after its checksum and its release-key signature match. It publishes Forgejo to the tailnet over HTTPS when Tailscale runs, creates an admin account with a host-local password, and dumps Forgejo every night with a copy of its secret files, which a restore needs. The `forgejo` profile switch that turns it on comes in a later change.
