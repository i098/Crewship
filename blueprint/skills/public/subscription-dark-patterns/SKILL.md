# Subscription dark patterns

Use when you design or review subscription pricing, renewal, cancellation, or reactivation flows.

A price that falls with each paid month can reward loyalty. Resetting it to the highest price after cancellation makes leaving costly and restarting punitive. The reel describes this as a retention tactic; it does not provide evidence for its claimed effect on retention.

Keep cancellation direct. Do not reset a returning customer's earned price or progress as a penalty. Make renewal terms and price changes clear before purchase.
