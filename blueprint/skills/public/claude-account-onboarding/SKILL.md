# Claude account onboarding: avoid suspension

Source: community reports that describe a hidden "ban score".
Anthropic has not confirmed them and nobody here has verified them.
Treat each rule as a cautious default, not a fact.
The reports say a new account tolerates less than an old one, and that an account below a hidden threshold is suspended.

## Before first login
- [ ] Use a clean, stable IP: no shared VPN, no proxy chain, no datacenter range if you can avoid it.
- [ ] Use one device or one host for the account. Do not log in from a second machine.
- [ ] Keep the same IP and device for the whole life of the account.
- [ ] Sign up with the real app or web flow once, by hand. Do not automate the signup.

## First days
- [ ] Log in once. Do not open parallel logins or parallel sessions.
- [ ] Start on a low plan. Do not buy the highest tier at the start.
- [ ] Use the account lightly for the first 2 days. Reports say this builds trust.
- [ ] Raise usage in small steps over days and weeks. Never jump from idle to the full limit.
- [ ] Do not burn the full usage limit in one burst. Spread the work across the day.
- [ ] If you run several accounts, rotate between them. Do not exhaust one account first.

## Every day
- [ ] Keep concurrency low. Do not start many agents on one account at once.
- [ ] Never retry a failed login in a loop. Back off and wait.
- [ ] Do not change IP, device or region without need.

## Warning sign: random logout
Reports call an unexpected logout a watch period: the account is near the threshold.
Do this at once:
1. Stop all automated use of that account. Do not log in again in a loop.
2. Wait. Reports say recovery is slow; allow days, not minutes.
3. Check the IP and device against the rules above. Fix any change you find.
4. Log in once, from the usual IP and device. Resume at a much lower pace and ramp up again.
5. If the account is suspended, use the official appeal. Do not make a new account from the same IP.

## Report
Tell the owner which items you could not meet (for example no clean IP). Do not guess.
